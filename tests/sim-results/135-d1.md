## How the lever interacts with the rules (rulebook 三: 控制 = 我方 ≥ 對方 + S, 上限 S + 2; 相印 = 楚控制國都 and at the cap)

Both capitals have S = 3, so the cap is 5.

- **Chu's free setup (南方 / 東方):** Chu may still put any of its 4 free points in 臨淄; Qin's point does not stop that (no adjacency limit at setup, and Qin with 1 does not control 臨淄). What changes is control: today 3 Chu points control 臨淄 (3 ≥ 0 + 3); with D1 they do not (3 < 1 + 3), 4 do. The bots put exactly 3 in 臨淄 in all 4,000 games, with or without D1. 薊 is outside Chu's free regions, so Qin's 1 there faces nobody at the start (and controls nothing: 1 < 0 + 3). Tested in `tests/d1-far-start.test.js`.
- **相印 (`sealAt: "cap"`):** Chu needs 5 in the capital and control, 5 ≥ Qin + 3, i.e. Qin ≤ 2. **Qin's 1 point alone changes nothing for 相印**: Chu needed 5 there anyway, and 5 ≥ 1 + 3. Only Qin at 3 or more blocks it while it stays (5 < 3 + 3), which is two more points than the foothold.
- **Control:** the foothold raises what Chu needs for plain control of 臨淄 / 薊 by one (3 → 4), and for Qin it opens placement under reach B in 臨淄, 薊 and their neighbours (即墨 莒 薛 中山 / 中山 代 遼東; today only 代 is in reach) and 遊說 there under realign-own once Chu is present (tested).

## Reading

1. **The foothold makes Chu take 臨淄's 相印 sooner and more often, not less.** 齊 相印 taken at some point: nn 40.2 → 69.8 % (+29.6 [23.7, 35.5]), hh 29.2 → 59.8 % (+30.6 [24.7, 36.5]); on turns 1–2 it goes from 45 to 236 games (nn) and from 30 to 193 (hh). The mechanism, from the two rules above: with D1, Chu's 3 setup points no longer control 臨淄, and the 2 points that win the control back (5 ≥ 1 + 3) are exactly the 2 that fill the cap. One 2-op placement buys the battleground back **and** the 相印 (+1 Mandate). Without D1, Chu already controls 臨淄 with 3 and those last 2 points compete with everything else. A probe on seeds 1–80 (nn, new vs new+D1): Chu places in 臨淄 during turn 1 in 38 vs 64 games, and the 齊 相印 comes right after a Chu placement in 14 vs 32 games (蘇秦's event 14 vs 20). Qin's foothold is rarely built into a block: Qin's mean influence in 臨淄 at turn ends is 1.37 (nn) / 1.08 (hh), and it is gone from 臨淄 at 22 % / 37 % of turn ends.
2. **合縱 endings go up**: +6.6 pp (nn, 33.2 → 39.8) and +6.8 pp (hh, 31.8 → 38.6), both significant. Qin's gains come from elsewhere: 天命 wins by Qin 62 → 80 (nn) and 38 → 56 (hh), 國都 wins by Qin 9 → 17 (nn) and 16 → 28 (hh). In nn 稱帝 drops 17.0 → 10.0 % (−7.0, significant; Qin's 稱帝 wins 61 → 37). The games are shorter: 5.31 → 4.94 (nn), 5.45 → 5.19 (hh).
3. **薊 works differently**: Chu has no setup points there, so the foothold survives (gone at 10 % of turn ends vs 87–90 % without D1) and mostly serves 滅燕: 燕 滅 in 5 → 20 games (nn) and 10 → 30 (hh). 燕 相印: +6.2 pp nn (30.0 → 36.2, the interval just leaves out 0), +0.4 pp hh.
4. **The 遊說 route the idea leans on is barely used.** With D1, Qin 遊說s in 齊 in 18 of 500 games (nn) and 26 (hh), 0.04–0.05 actions a game; in 燕 in 15 / 30 games. Qin places more there (齊: 1.01 → 1.80 points a game nn, 1.10 → 1.65 hh; 燕: 0.30 → 0.85, 0.39 → 1.11) and campaigns there a little less. Chu 遊說s 齊 more (0.08 → 0.25 a game nn): the foothold is a target for Chu's realign-own too.
5. **Capitals:** 關中 fell in 18.2 → 17.6 % of games (nn) and 22.0 → 17.4 % (hh, −4.6 [−9.5, 0.3]); 郢 8.8 → 8.8 % and 10.8 → 12.0 %. Neither change is significant. 國都 endings 39 → 54 (nn) and 59 → 55 (hh).
6. **Level split:** D1 is worth +2.6 pp at nn and +6.8 pp at hh; the gap between the two is inside the noise at 500 games.
7. **today + D1 does not break today's game** (0 errors, 0 stuck; win rate −2.4 pp nn and +2.8 pp hh, neither significant), but the same 臨淄 effect shows: 齊 相印 +34.0 pp (nn) and +26.6 pp (hh), 合縱 endings +8.8 pp (nn, significant), 一統 −4.6 pp (nn, significant).
8. **Seed for seed**, D1 flips many games both ways (nn new: 96 Qin → Chu and 109 Chu → Qin out of 500; hh new: 92 and 126). The lever is not a small nudge on a few games. It reshuffles the opening in 臨淄 and nets out to a few points.

The lever undershoots. To reach the band's lower edge Qin still needs about +8.7 pp at nn and +4.5 pp at hh on top of D1 (+11.2 / +7.0 to reach 50). As the brief asks, I tried no other lever.

## What I did not verify

- **Bots, not people.** The bots are one-ply. A human Qin may spend 2 ops on turn 1 to bring 臨淄 to 3 and block its 相印 for good, which the bot rarely does (mean 1.37 there). A human Chu will certainly see the 2-op control + 相印 in 臨淄. So the direction of item 1 should hold for people; its size may not.
- **Qin at 3 or more in 臨淄** at turn ends (the block) is not a column. I report the mean and the gone share only.
- **`lbRem` / `lbLost` (the #130 columns) undercount under `realign-own`.** `lobbyStats` reads the `lobby` entry when it first appears, but under realign-own the entry's `removed` / `lost` keep growing with later attempts, one choice per roll. A probe on 20 nn games gave 144 at first sight against 163 at the end, and 163 from the per-attempt `realign` entries. It is not fixed here (#130's columns, out of scope). The D1 columns count removals from the `realign` entries, so they are not affected.
