## Do the bots read `options.seals`?

Yes. Nothing that decides a move hard-codes 4.
- **#134's win by placement** (`bots.js` `winningPlacements`): `Object.keys(st.seals).length >= st.options.seals - 1`.
- **`evaluate`'s alliance term:** `2 * seals + (seals === st.options.seals - 1 ? 8 : 0)`. The +8 "one marker from the win" step moves to 4 held.
- **The advisor** (`advisor.js`) takes its seal buckets from `evaluate` (`d("seals")`, `sealRoad:*`).
- **The engine's win check** reads `st.options.seals`. `board.js`'s `SEALS_TO_WIN = 4` is exported, but nothing reads it.
- **One term does not scale with the option:** the seal-road weight `sealScale = 1 + 0.7 × seals held`. It grows with the markers held, not with the markers still missing. Under seals 5, a road at 2–3 held is weighed as under seals 4, where 3 held is one short. So both bots value 相印 roads a little above their new distance to the win: Chu chases them a bit harder than it should, and Qin defends them a bit harder. Which way that moves Qin's win rate is unclear, and it is small next to the +8 step, which does move. Not changed.
- **Not in the sims, but hard-coded 4 in player-facing files** (FE / writer), if seals 5 is adopted:
  - `app.js:1253`, the stat line `${seals} / 4`;
  - `i18n` `ends.alliance` "four seals held" / 「四國相印」, and the ends lists (en 268, zh-Hant 225);
  - `rules.js` 49, 499, 504, 526.

  `app.js:3346` and `audio-cues.js:201` already read the option.
